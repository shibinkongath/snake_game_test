### **How to Play**

* Control snake with WASD / Arrow Keys.
* Scoring breakdown (Normal Food: +10, Bonus Food: +20).
* Hitting snake's head on the boundaries or its own body leads to Game over.



#### What Was Added Beyond Basic Snake:

* Smooth interpolated movement.
* Path-based body following.
* Dynamic speed increase with cap.
* Persistent Top 4 high scores (JSON).
* Object pooling for body segments.
* Turn queue with neck-collision guard.
* Grid-aligned food spawning with occupancy checks.
